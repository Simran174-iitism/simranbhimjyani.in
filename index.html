About

About Me

I am a Research Scholar at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad specialising in Digital Humanities, with a focus on eco-computational approaches to Indian literary texts. My work sits at the intersection of technology, environment, and literature — exploring how digital methods can open new ways of reading and understanding nature in Indian writing.

🏫 IIT (ISM) Dhanbad ◆ HASTAC Scholar ◆ Advisory Member, GDHS ◆ Member, DHARTI
